The Good

Transportation and Things to Do

In Jefferson Park, there are many local businesses and parks in the vicinity that provide a space for leisure and relaxation. The transportation in Jefferson Park furthermore makes getting to these destinations easier. Whether you are a tourist or a resident, there's always something fun and some way to get there.

Transportation

Jefferson Park, being the major transportation hub that it is, provides a variety of transportation methods into the neighborhood. Should you be in Downtown Chicago, you can take the Union Pacific Northwest Metra from Chicago OTC or the CTA Blue Line. If you are arriving from the airport, you can take the Blue line or taxi to Jefferson Park. The multitude of bus lines also makes getting from the train to your final destination a breeze.

Jefferson Park Transit Center

Should you prefer to drive, the Kennedy Expressway is your best friend. The neighborhood provides easy access to the highway which makes commuting to and from work and school easier.

Entrance onto I90 at Jefferson Park

Veterans Square

Once you arrive in this vibrant neighborhood, there is a small square right near the transportation center which hosts options for a quick meal. Should it be early, you can grab a coffee at the Dunkin' Donuts, late in the afternoon you can grab a sandwich at Subway, and if you just want some chips and a drink the 7/11 is a great option. The square has a variety of businesses which allows people in the area to get a haircut and dryclean their clothes. The walk is a nice and simple three minutes!

The short walk from the transit center to the square

Jefferson Park Public Library and Park

Jefferson Park, like most neighborhoods, has its own public library where kids and adults can come to read books, use the computers and printers, and be involved in community activities. While I have not visited in a long time, the library did have a children's play area which featured giant Lego blocks. Whenever I would visit, I would build a small fortress with the blocks and have fun with the other children who visited.

Jefferson Park Public Library

Just across the street and a little to the West is the Jefferson Memorial Park. The park has many ammenities including a pool, playground, tennis courts, and baseball field. There is also a Sunday farmers' market that takes place on certain dates. (A link to their website can be found here: jeffersonparksundaymarket.com) The park is a great place for meet and greets and in the summer you can often see children running around, groups playing tennis and pickleball, and many swimming in the pool when it's open.

Jefferson Memorial Park

Copernicus Center

The Copernicus Center is a theater which is also home to the largest Polish festival in the country: Taste of Polonia. Taste of Polonia runs once a year and has about 35,000 visitors. Many artists and dancers are involved in the event and there is good Polish food served.
